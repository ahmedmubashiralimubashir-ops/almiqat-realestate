🔥 Firebase & GitHub Pages Configuration

1. Instant Local Persistence (Active Now):
All your changes (texts, prices, new properties, inquiries) are automatically saved to your browser's persistent store and reflected instantly in the live DOM with zero redeployment needed.

2. Linking with Google Firebase Authentication & Firestore:
To sync updates across different computers and staff accounts simultaneously:

  • Create a free Firebase project at console.firebase.google.com.
  • Enable Email/Password Authentication under Authentication → Sign-in method.
  • Enable Cloud Firestore in production/test mode.
  • Add your Firebase configuration credentials to main.js or index.html.

3. GitHub Pages Deployment:
Upload your updated index.html to your repository at:
https://github.com/ahmedmubashiralimubashir-ops/almiqat-realestate/upload/main
